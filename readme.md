# Privacy Policy — Arrow Puzzle: Arrows Escape

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains how the Android app **Arrow Puzzle: Arrows Escape** (package `com.olfoxacin.arrowpuzzlemobile`, "the app") handles information.

## Information we collect
We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not send your game data to us. Your progress, stars and settings are saved only on your device.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
If you have not bought "Remove ads", the app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:
- your device's advertising ID
- your IP address and approximate location
- device and app information (model, OS version, language)
- how you interact with ads

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in some US states, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings > Privacy & ad choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)
The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation that the purchase exists, so it can turn ads off.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
