# Privacy Policy — Arrow Puzzle: Arrows Escape

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android app **Arrow Puzzle: Arrows Escape** (package `com.olfoxacin.arrowpuzzlemobile`, "the app") handles information.

## Information we collect
We (the developer) do not sell any personal information. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and your progress, stars and settings are saved only on your device. The app uses two Google services, described below: Google AdMob for ads and Google Analytics for Firebase for anonymous usage statistics.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
If you have not bought "Remove ads", the app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:
- your device's advertising ID
- your IP address and approximate location
- device and app information (model, OS version, language)
- how you interact with ads

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in some US states, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings > Privacy & ad choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)
To understand how the game is played and how ads perform, so we can improve it, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It may collect:
- an app-instance ID and your device's advertising ID
- your IP address (masked) and approximate location derived from it
- game events such as app opens, sessions, levels started and completed
- ad impressions and estimated ad revenue, and in-app purchase events (product and price; never payment details)
- device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)

This data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and Google's privacy policy. We only see aggregated reports and do not use it to identify you. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)
The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation that the purchase exists, so it can turn ads off.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app removes everything it stores on your device. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage Google ad data at https://myadcenter.google.com. To ask us to request deletion of analytics data linked to your device, email us at the address below.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
